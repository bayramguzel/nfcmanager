## 🇺🇸 ENGLISH

### 📌 App Name
NFC Manager – Read & Analyze NFC Cards

### 💬 Short Description *(max 80 characters)*
📡 Read and analyze NFC cards & tags on your Android device with ease!

### 📖 Full Description

🔍 Discover the hidden world of NFC technology with **NFC Manager**!

Read a wide variety of NFC-enabled cards including identity cards, bank cards, transportation cards, and access cards. Get detailed technical information about each scanned card or tag.

✨ **Key Features:**

- 📡 **Wide Card Support** — Identity, bank, transportation, access cards and more
- 🔍 **Detailed Technical Info** — Card type, UID, protocol, and data structures
- 🔒 **Privacy Protected** — No card data transmitted to external servers
- ⚡ **No External Reader** — Uses your device's built-in NFC hardware
- 📱 **User-Friendly Interface** — Technical and user data shown in clear, readable format
- 🎓 **Education & Research** — Ideal for learning about NFC technology

⚠️ Sensitive data (bank balances, personal details) from encrypted/protected cards cannot be read. Only non-encrypted technical information is displayed.

🏷️ **Keywords:** NFC reader, card analysis, NFC tag, near field communication, RFID
