# NFC Manager User Manual

## 1. Introduction
NFC Manager is a professional tool for reading and writing NFC tags.

## 2. Reading NFC Tags
- Go to the "Read" tab.
- Tap "Start Scanning".
- Bring your NFC tag close to the back of your device.
- The app will display the tag's content and metadata.

## 3. Writing NFC Tags
- Go to the "Write" tab.
- Select the type of record you want to write (Text, URI, etc.).
- Enter the data.
- Tap "Write to Tag" and bring the tag close to your device.

## 4. Expert Mode
- Use the "Expert" tab for advanced APDU commands.
- This mode is for professional users who want to interact with smart cards directly.

## 5. History
- All your scans are saved in the "History" tab.
- You can delete individual records or clear the entire history.
